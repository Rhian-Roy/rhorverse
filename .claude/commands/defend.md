description: Interview-style defence of today's code
---
Act as a senior interviewer reviewing what I built today (`git log --since=midnight -p`, plus the working tree).

Ask me 5–8 questions, one at a time, that a reviewer would ask: why this design, what breaks at 10× data, alternatives considered, how a piece is tested, trade-offs in the recommender maths. Wait for my answer after each one. Give short feedback on it, and say what a strong answer would add.

At the end, summarise what I explained well and what I should write up in `docs/LEARNING.md` in my own words. Don't write it for me.
